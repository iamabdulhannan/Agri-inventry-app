# Delete your ZaraiBook account

You can delete your ZaraiBook account and its data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open **ZaraiBook** and sign in.
2. Go to **More → Settings**.
3. Scroll to **Delete account**, tap **Delete my account**, type **DELETE** and confirm.

If you own a shop that has other team members, remove them first under **More → Team**, then delete your account.

## Without the app

Send an email to **iamabdalhannan@gmail.com** from the email address you signed up with, with the subject **"Delete my ZaraiBook account"**. We will delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted

- Your login, name and email.
- If you are the only member of a shop: the shop and all of its records (products, stock, purchases, sales, khata, roznamcha, bank, investors, reports).

## What is kept

- Shops that have other members keep their business records. Entries you made remain, without your name.
- Removed data may stay in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before it is erased.
