# ZaraiBook Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

ZaraiBook ("the app") helps agricultural input shops (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides) keep their stock, sales invoices, customer and supplier accounts (khata), cash book (roznamcha), bank records and reports. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Data we collect

**Account data.** When you create an account we store your name, email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).

**Business data you enter.** Everything you type into the app is stored so that you and your team can use it: shop name, address, phone, NTN/STRN, products, stock, purchases, sales invoices, customer and supplier names and phone numbers, payments, cheques, bank account names and numbers you choose to record, crop advances, investors and notes.

**Data kept on your phone.** The app keeps your sign-in session, language and theme choices, and a copy of recent data on the device so it opens quickly and works when the internet is slow.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising ID. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

## How we use the data

Only to run the app for you: showing your shop's records to you and the team members you invite, printing and sharing invoices you choose to share, and keeping your account secure. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone for marketing.

## Who can see your data

- **You and your team.** Data belongs to a shop. Only people who are members of that shop can see it, with the role you give them (owner, admin, staff).
- **Our service providers.** Data is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com) (database and sign-in), which processes it on our behalf under its own security and privacy terms.
- **People you share with.** When you share an invoice (PDF, WhatsApp or text), it goes to the person and app you pick.
- **Legal reasons.** We may disclose data if required by law.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access in the database is limited by shop membership and role. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

## Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time:

- **In the app:** More → Settings → Delete account.
- **Without the app:** see [How to delete your account](delete-account.md).

When you delete your account, your login and profile are removed. If you are the only member of a shop, that shop and all its records are deleted too. Shops that have other members keep their business records (entries you made stay, without your name). Copies in backups are removed within 30 days.

## Children

The app is a business tool and is not intended for children under 13.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
